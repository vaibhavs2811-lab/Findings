## Phase Goal

**As a** researcher, **I want to** switch My Matches to Mentorship and get ranked mentors (if I am seeking one) or mentees (if I am open to mentoring), with what each side gives and gets, **so that** the mentoring exchange works in both directions.

This plan's slice: Mentorship mode works end to end in both directions with the right people in it. Mentors and mentees are filtered by toggles and career stage in the database, ranked by profile similarity, cached per mode, and gated by the viewer's own toggles. Plan 06-02 adds the two-way give/need ranking, the Gemini two-sided explanations and the give/need-aware fallback on top of the same path.

**Execution prerequisite:** Phases 2, 3, 4 and 5 must have been executed first. This plan extends Phase 4's `findings/services/matching.py` (`get_matches`, `MatchResult`, the cache ladder) and `views/matches.py`, and reuses Phase 5's `ui/connect_button.py`.
